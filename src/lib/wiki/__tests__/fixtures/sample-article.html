A Study of Widgets & Gadgets

This paper introduces a new method for widget analysis. The approach uses kernel smoothing and "robust" estimators, requiring that x < y for all comparisons, achieving results that surpass prior work by a wide margin across every benchmark we tested, including several held-out datasets that were never seen during development or tuning.

We evaluate on three datasets and report results in Table 1. Our method consistently outperforms baselines,
with the largest gains observed on the noisiest data. This finding held across every random seed we tried, and the effect size was large enough to be practically significant, not merely statistically detectable at the usual thresholds researchers rely on.

Related Work
